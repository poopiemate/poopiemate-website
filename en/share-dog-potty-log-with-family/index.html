How to Share Your Dog's Potty Log with Family in Real Time

The easiest way is to use an app made for sharing potty logs. One person logs it with a single tap, the rest of the family gets notified right away, and it's saved by date.

How it compares to the usual ways

① Sharing photos in a KakaoTalk group chat (or any family group chat)

This is the most common way. But after just a few days, you're scrolling through the chat to answer "What time did they last go yesterday?" You also have to figure out the time between potty breaks yourself.

② Writing it down in a notes app or calendar

Only the person who wrote it can see it. So it's hard to know in real time whether someone else in the family already took the dog out for a walk or potty break.

③ A potty log sharing app (like POOPIEMATE)

Your family shares one dog profile, and no matter who logs it, everyone gets notified right away. The Poopie Gauge shows how long it's been since the last record.

Especially helpful when

  • You're two working parents and the person caring for the dog changes during the day
  • You share dog care with parents or siblings in a bigger household
  • You're a multi-dog household with two or more dogs (set up separate profiles and share each one)
  • Your dog only goes potty outside, so only the person who did the walk knows if they went

How to start sharing with family in POOPIEMATE

  1. Install POOPIEMATE for free on iOS or Android.
  2. Create your dog's profile and add family members with an invite link.
  3. Log poop, pee and walks with one tap.
  4. No matter who logs it, the rest of the family gets notified right away, and records build up by date in the Records tab.

FAQ

Can several family members share one log?

Yes. Your whole family shares one profile per dog. No matter who logs it, it goes into the same history and everyone gets notified.

If I have more than one dog, can I share each one separately?

Yes. Multi-dog households can set up a separate profile for each dog and share each one with family.

Is it free?
