FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PoopieMate?

PoopieMate is a dog care app where family members share potty and walk records in real time. Whoever logs it, everyone in the family is notified instantly — plus potty interval alerts, a weather-based walk safety score, and routine time settings.

Can my whole family see the dog's potty records?

Yes. With the family invite feature, multiple caregivers can share one dog profile. Each family member's records update in real time, so everyone can check potty and walk status together.

We're both working — can we split walk and potty duty and still log it together?

Yes. Even when family members log at different times, it all updates in real time on one shared dog profile. You can instantly see who walked and logged today, which makes splitting duty easy for dual-income households and larger families.

Is this app good for outdoor-potty dogs like Jindo mixes?

Yes, PoopieMate is designed for outdoor-potty dogs like Jindo mixes and other mixed breeds. Log potty events during walks, track potty intervals, and get routine reminders to manage outdoor potty habits systematically. It works just as well for indoor-potty dogs too.

How is the walk safety score calculated?

It's a 1-5 score based on your dog's profile — body size, whether they're brachycephalic (short-nosed), coat type, age group, and heart or respiratory conditions — combined with real-time feels-like temperature and rain probability. Brachycephalic or heavy-coated dogs score higher risk on hot days; short-coated or cold-sensitive dogs score higher risk on cold days. Designed with heatstroke-prevention guidelines for dogs in mind.
