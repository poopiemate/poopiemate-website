Our white Jindo mix, once called “Bunhong-i” at a kennel by a farm field, is family now. She never goes indoors, so she walks three times a day — and as a working couple we kept calling to ask “Did she poop?” So we built an app where one tap tells the whole family.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can the whole family use it together?

Yes. Send an invite code by message and everyone logs the same dog. Whoever logs it, everyone else gets an alert right away. Invites, logs and alerts are all free.

We both work. Can we split walk and potty duty?

Yes. Logs from different people at different times land on one shared dog profile in real time. You can see who walked today, and on busy days ask someone else to take the walk with one button.

Does it work for dogs that only go outside?

Yes. PoopieMate was built by a family whose dog only goes on walks, never indoors. Logging on walks, potty gauges and walk reminders keep that routine on track. Indoor-trained dogs work just as well.

Can I add more than one dog?

Yes. Add as many dogs as you have and switch between them from the top of the screen. Family members can follow each dog too.

How is the walk safety score calculated?

It combines your dog’s profile — body type, short-nosed breed, coat, age, heart or breathing conditions — with live feels-like temperature and rain chance into a 1–5 score. It follows heatstroke-prevention guidance for dogs.
