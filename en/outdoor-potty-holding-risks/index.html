Is It Bad for Dogs to Hold Their Pee and Poop?Risks and Tips

Dogs that only go outside simply hold it when they can't get a walk. Now and then is fine, but if long holds keep happening, they can lead to problems like bladder infections, urinary stones and constipation, so it's best to give them several potty chances a day.

01Why holding it is hard on the body

Pee flushes waste and bacteria out of the body. When it sits for a long time, bacteria can multiply more easily, and minerals in the urine have more time to clump together. Poop also loses water and gets harder the longer it stays in the bowel.

02Problems that can come from holding it too long

ProblemHow it happensSigns to look for
Urinary tract infection / bladder infectionBacteria can multiply in urine that sits.Trying to go often but only a little comes out, blood in the urine, a change in smell
Urinary stonesMinerals in concentrated urine can form crystals or stones. Drinking little water makes this more likely.Straining with nothing coming out, blood in the urine, signs of pain
Constipation / impacted stoolPoop that stays in the bowel a long time can get hard.Small, hard poops, straining for a long time
Stretched bladderHolding it for very long stretches again and again can strain the bladder muscle.Not emptying fully, leaking urine
Strain on the kidneysIf the urinary tract gets blocked or an infection spreads upward, it can affect the kidneys too.Low energy, vomiting, drinking unusually large amounts of water
Good to know

This table is general reference information, not a diagnosis. If your dog strains and not a single drop of urine comes out, the urethra may be blocked, so go to the vet right away. Blood in the urine, vomiting or a limp, listless dog also need a vet right away.

03How many hours can a dog hold it? (common guidelines)

Age / conditionCommon guidelineNotes
PuppyAbout their age in months + 1 hourAround 4 hours at 3 months. Taking them out more often is still better.
AdultA chance at least once every 6 to 8 hoursAbout 3 to 5 potty chances a day is the common advice.
Senior or dogs with health conditionsShorter than adultsBladder control can weaken, so let them out more often.

Dogs may hold it longer overnight while sleeping, but it's best to avoid a run of days with no chance to go for more than 8 hours during the day. To find your dog's usual interval from your logs, see how often a dog should poop and potty intervals.

04What to do on rainy, stormy or freezing days

  • Go out, even briefly: you don't need a long walk. Stepping out front just to do their business is enough. Have a raincoat and a towel for paws ready.
  • Don't push it on dangerous days: on dangerous days like typhoons, heavy rain or cold-wave warnings, check the dog walk safety index first, and use sheltered spots like under an awning or a parking garage entrance.
  • Use an indoor pad as backup: teaching your dog to use an indoor pad ahead of time makes these days much less stressful. How to do it is in why do dogs only potty outside?.
  • Don't cut back on water: limiting water so they pee less actually makes urinary problems more likely.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many hours can a dog hold their pee?

The common guideline is about their age in months + 1 hour for puppies, and a chance at least once every 6 to 8 hours for adults.
Every dog is different, so it helps to know your dog's usual interval from your logs.

My outdoor-only dog hasn't gone all day. Is that okay?

Dogs often hold it because of the weather, but holding it a long time is hard on the body.
Take them out even briefly, and if they strain and nothing comes out, go to the vet right away.

What illnesses can holding pee cause?

It can make urinary tract infections, bladder infections and urinary stones more likely, and in serious cases it can strain the kidneys.
Poop can harden and lead to constipation.

Can I skip the walk on a rainy day?

You can skip the long walk, but still give them a potty chance.
You can step out front briefly just to do their business, or use an indoor pad as a backup.
